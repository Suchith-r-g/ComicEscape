## 🎮 Concept & Core Mechanics
- **Genre:** 3D First-Person Escape Room
- **Engine:** Godot 4.x (WebGL / Compatibility)
- **Visuals:** High-contrast cel-shading, halftone patterns, and comic speech/sound billboards.
- **Hero Mechanic:** Toggle gravity to float gently upward, bypassing floor hazards and reaching elevated panels.
- **The Meta-Twist:** After 15 minutes of solving puzzles to retrieve *Comic Book Issue #1*, the player discovers the exit door was unlocked from the very start.
